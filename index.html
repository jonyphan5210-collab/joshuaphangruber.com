Welcome! I'm Josh. I spent my twenties as an actor and singer, then took an unusual path through an MBA to working in climate. When I'm not working I'm running D&D campaigns, hiking in national parks, playing guitar, and reading about the Mongols or some other dead empire. This site is where I keep track of what I'm reading, listening to, and thinking about.
